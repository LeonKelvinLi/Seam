# Dark media direction — scope

Request: brainstorm a darker Clipmelt homepage, use substantially less white,
make the experience more media-led, and add stronger 3D and scroll animation.

Audit the deployed homepage at https://clipmelt.com and its current source,
src/components/CinematicHome.tsx and CinematicHome.module.css at aeff5d6.
Primary user: a creator/editor deciding whether to connect two existing shots.
Primary task: understand AI transitions, see a convincing example, then create
a transition. Brand remains Clipmelt. Airplane window-to-mountains remains the
featured scroll example. Retain native page scrolling and motion controls.

This pass produces a concrete visual brainstorm, evidence, and implementation
handoff. No production design changes are included in this planning pass.
Interpret “media-like” as a cinematic editing/screening environment, with real
footage carrying the visual identity. No competitor or external style reference
was supplied. Existing typography may stay; prioritize palette, composition,
and choreography. Use Google Chrome only for browser evidence.
