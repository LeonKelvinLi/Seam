# Dark media brainstorm

## Three directions considered

| Direction | Composition | Motion | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark cinema | Huge continuous footage, small overlay type | Frame grows to fill screen | Use for airplane chapter |
| Editing suite | Clear message beside layered source/result media | Planes assemble into one cut | Recommended foundation |
| Screening gallery | Near-black editorial reel, wide examples | Perspective filmstrip opens on scroll | Use for examples |

One continuous dark environment combines these moments without reskinning each
section. Real media supplies color; shadows and scale supply depth.

Palette: cinema #050608; charcoal #11151b; edge #28303b; silver #d4d8df;
secondary #949dab; iris #ab9af2. Explore cold silver or warm copper as small
accent alternatives. Avoid large white/lavender surfaces. Text remains readable,
not dimmed into the background. Existing Space Grotesk/DM Sans remain appropriate.
Use a compact spacing scale8/12/16/24/32/48/72; body16px, details>=12px.

## Scroll choreography

1. Assemble: two original footage planes sit at opposing angles. On scroll they
   approach the same editing axis; the generated center result advances in Z.
   The copy and CTA stay stationary, with no delayed entrance blocking actions.
2. Fly through: the airplane result frame rotates flat and expands toward the
   viewport. Once readable edge-to-edge, the existing eased scroll playback
   takes over. Caption moves out of the frame only when no longer needed.
3. Inspect: the process shows actual source and transition thumbnails in depth.
   Scrolling aligns them into a single timeline, revealing the three steps.
4. Choose: example frames fan forward into a readable filmstrip; hover/focus
   introduces a slight tilt and enables preview on deliberate interaction.
5. Finish: dark closing shot, compact message and stable create action.

## Plan critique

Black plus purple alone is generic. The distinctive content must be the user's
two shots, actual generated middle frames, and the airplane-window transition.
Remove generic orbital shapes rather than multiplying them. More motion comes
from larger coherent movement and changing perspective, not constant agitation.
Keep the media clear and bright within its frame; darken chrome and backgrounds.

## Concept and limits

An interactive in-conversation composition tests dark palette, three sample
pairs and adjustable depth/scroll position using existing still frames. It is a
storyboard, not a generated video or deployed page. The live site is unchanged
by this brainstorming pass. Real scroll expansion and synchronized playback
belong in the subsequent implementation.
